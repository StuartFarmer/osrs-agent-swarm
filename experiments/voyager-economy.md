# Voyager economy experiment

The common task across generations is to make money in a shared RuneScape world. Ability describes the character's stats and resources. Personality describes preferences and morals, including trust, reciprocity and willingness to deceive in game. Occupation describes the work the character actually does. A miner can become a broker within one lifetime. A descendant can inherit its parent's approach to finding work and still choose a different occupation.

## Sequence

| Stage | Setup | Question |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Common prompt | Many headless agents in one world, identical policy text, model, starting area, stats, kit, public SDK guide and empty private skill libraries. Give everyone the same wealth objective and opportunity to talk, trade and teach. | Which occupations, partnerships and wealth differences arise from play alone? |
| 2. Starting conditions | Keep the common prompt. Add operator setup for an exact spawn or seeded sampling from valid walkable tiles, predetermined skill levels, inventory, equipped gear and cash. | How much do geography, abilities and capital explain? |
| 3. Personality | Hold starting conditions comparable and vary private preferences. An environment-informed LLM can render sampled traits into prompts. Freeze the exact text and record assignments. Honesty can be a trait, including willingness to deceive for in-game gain. | Which personalities form partnerships, honor agreements or earn more under comparable conditions? |
| 4. Evolution | Select parent policies across completed runs. A prompt optimizer or an LLM creates child policies through mutation or recombination. Give descendants fresh characters in comparison worlds. | Does each generation earn more on fresh trials? Which dispositions and occupations persist across lineages? |

For stage 1, the RuneBench prompt and skill tools implement the Voyager loop of choosing an immediate task, retrieving relevant routines, executing, checking observed effects, repairing failures and saving useful programs. Keep personal libraries discoverable by their owners. Agents can choose to teach one another. The existing [25-agent preset](headless-25.json) provides a starting roster. Verify actual prompt hashes, observed initial holdings, active character counts and decision opportunities before interpreting a large run. Differences in model scheduling can look like differences in initiative.

For stage 2, apply assignments once before the first decision, verify the resulting position, XP and assets, and store both requested and observed conditions in the run manifest. Resuming a run must preserve progress. Compare location alone, stats alone and loadout alone before combining them. Measure wealth gain from each agent's actual endowment. Allow travel after placement so geography can influence who discovers whom.

For stage 3, pair runs on the same starting assignments and rotate traits across characters. Score conduct from offers, fulfillment, transfers and subsequent partner choices. A failed delivery can have several explanations. Record uncertain cases. Measure specialization through repeated activities, outputs, earnings sources and counterparties. Record an occupational preference separately from the occupation observed.

For stage 4, choose a first inheritance rule that is easy to interpret. Pass down private personality text and occupational strategy. Give the optimizer parent policies and evidence of their behavior and earnings. Each child records its parent revision IDs, generation, exact inherited and edited text, optimizer version and evaluation worlds. A mutation can have one parent and a recombination two. Keep acquired stats, money and reputation with the parent. Test inheritance of learned Voyager routines as a separate comparison using exact skill revisions. This distinguishes learning during a lifetime from intergenerational improvement.

Select on wealth gain across replicated worlds. Re-evaluate descendants, their parents and the unchanged founding prompt under matched endowments, elapsed game time and held-out world and counterpart assignments. Track the distribution of earnings and Gini of wealth levels over time, plus aggregate wealth, role persistence and partner networks. The interacting world is the replication unit. Include a contemporaneous founding-policy control because the profitability of a strategy depends on who else adopts it.

## What can be sold

The pinned Lost City 274 server includes a custom Grand Exchange. Its engine defaults GE_ENABLED to true. Our world launcher uses the engine default unless overridden. The table is at (3180, 3443) inside Varrock west bank on the ground floor. Stand at (3181, 3443), or use the teller at (3181, 3445). The game HTTP endpoint GET /api/market/status or sdk.getGEAvailability() reports availability for a particular world. Record this at run start together with initial bids and trading activity. Physical offer placement and collection require a visit. Public quote reads are available remotely. [Market guide](../vendor/rs-sdk/sdk/MARKET.md) · [Engine setting](../vendor/rs-sdk/server/engine/src/util/Environment.ts) · [Launcher](../src/world.ts)

Use the character's **estimated net sale proceeds** plus owned coins as its liquidation wealth. Primary fitness is the change from start to finish. Secondary outcomes are realized cash flows, the wealth trajectory and its time average. An agent can be a successful gatherer during a period of low demand, so retain quantities carried and produced alongside GP.

Define the valuation convention before a run. For a first version, estimate proceeds once goods reach a permitted venue at the sampled tick. This assumes access to the venue and ignores travel time. Actual sales capture travel, delay and changing prices. Trace ownership across backpack, equipment, bank, open offers and uncollected claims. Count refundable coins and recoverable items once. A stale bank observation makes total holdings uncertain.

Simulate sales against outstanding eligible bids in price/time order, excluding the seller's bids and respecting remaining quantities. Apply the engine's cumulative 5% seller tax with integer rounding. The public quote gives the best bid and aggregate quantity. Exact proceeds for a stack require an observer snapshot of executable bids by price, owner and remaining size. Use a read-only consistent snapshot so valuation never consumes demand in the live world. [Quote fields](../vendor/rs-sdk/sdk/market.ts) · [Matching and tax](../vendor/rs-sdk/server/engine/src/engine/market/MarketStore.ts)

For example, if another agent bids 10 GP for three logs and there are no other bids, only three of ten logs can immediately sell there. The sale grosses 30 GP and yields 29 GP after tax on a fresh offer. Report seven unmatched logs. If a reachable NPC shop buys logs, compare its executable proceeds, including price changes with stock, and allocate units across venues once. Until that path is measured, label the result GE-only. An observed empty book gives zero immediately executable GE proceeds. An unavailable book gives an unknown value. The market has no artificial buyers or stock. [GE maintenance notes](../vendor/rs-sdk/docs/grand-exchange.md)

Each agent's estimate asks what that agent could realize acting individually. Several sellers can depend on the same bid, so summing their estimates overstates what everyone could cash out together. Report individual inequality using the declared convention. For aggregate comparisons, use common fixed prices as a supplementary inventory measure or simulate a joint liquidation with an explicit ordering. Keep gifts and trade transfers visible as redistribution, and market tax as a sink.

## Connecting the private GE to OSRS prices

The common-prompt headless run captures the Wiki/RuneLite feed as an observer reference for matched items. The JSON base path is `https://prices.runescape.wiki/api/v1/osrs`. The latest endpoint reports observed high and low transaction prices with separate timestamps. The five-minute and hourly endpoints provide averages and volumes on each side. A reported sale establishes a historical price. A buyer must still have an outstanding bid and sufficient coins for a sale in the private world. [Wrapper source](https://github.com/nyan-left/runelite-grand-exchange-prices/blob/main/src/api-wrapper.ts)

| Experiment | Use of OSRS data | What the comparison measures |
|---|---|---|
| Common-prompt baseline | Observer values each character's mapped inventory at the same frozen OSRS sell-side prices from the start of the run. Report this alongside local liquidation wealth and cash. | How local economic outcomes compare with the value of the goods produced at a common set of external prices. |
| Price information | Give every agent the same timestamped OSRS bulletin. Agents can consult it while negotiating and placing ordinary local orders. | Whether a shared reference changes spreads, trade frequency, specialization or trust. Local prices may converge or diverge. |
| External liquidity | A separately funded market maker posts real bids and asks through the private GE around the external sell and buy references. Orders compete under the existing price/time rules. | How behavior changes when outside demand and supply become executable. Report agent-to-agent and maker trades separately. |

For comparability, fetch in bulk once per update on the host, use a descriptive User-Agent, and record the payload, observation times, retrieval time and item mapping version. Map the local tradeable catalogue to the Wiki mapping by verified item identity, resolving noted forms to the underlying item and checking variants and stack units. Flag ambiguous items, missing prices and stale observations and report coverage. The same snapshot or historical sequence can be replayed in paired worlds. Reveal each observation only when its data would have been available at the corresponding simulation time. [Mapping and prices](https://github.com/nyan-left/runelite-grand-exchange-prices#usage) · [Local catalogue](../vendor/rs-sdk/server/engine/src/engine/market/MarketCatalog.ts)

For the market-maker treatment, record starting cash and stock, spread, replenishment policy and order sizes in the experiment manifest. Calibrate sizes to the private population and inspect how much demand other agents still supply. Public OSRS volume measures completed trades and gives no executable depth for our world. Withdraw stale or crossed quotes. Record maker inventory, cash flows and local seller tax so money supplied by the maker is visible in the accounts. For evolution comparisons, replay the same external series for founders, parents and children and test on held-out periods.

## Within-run evolution

The death-triggered crossover treatment runs alongside the original common-prompt baseline. Selection uses current wealth, and within-life performance uses gain over each individual’s own measured endowment. The engine preserves loot for survivors. Compare lineages at equal ages as well as on the calendar-time scoreboard. See the [implementation and launch instructions](../HEADLESS.md#permanent-death-experiment).
